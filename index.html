Air Pollution Awareness

Air pollution is one of the biggest environmental threats. This project helps people understand air quality and take action for a cleaner and healthier future.

Why Air Pollution Awareness is Important?

Air pollution affects human health, animals, climate, and the environment. Awareness helps people change habits and reduce pollution.

How Can We Reduce Air Pollution?

  • Use public transport and carpooling
  • Plant more trees
  • Use clean and renewable energy
  • Reduce factory and vehicle emissions
  • Spread awareness in schools and communities
