Introduction to Air Pollution

Air pollution is the contamination of air by harmful gases, smoke, dust and chemicals. It is one of the most serious environmental problems in the world today.

Breathing polluted air causes asthma, lung diseases and heart problems. Clean air is essential for healthy life.

Why Air Pollution Awareness is Important

Many people are not aware of how daily activities increase air pollution. Awareness helps people understand the impact on health and environment.

Educating students and communities is the first step towards a clean and sustainable future.

Causes and Effects of Air Pollution

  • Vehicle emissions
  • Industrial smoke
  • Burning garbage and plastics
  • Firecrackers
  • Deforestation

Air pollution leads to global warming, acid rain, poor visibility and damage to crops and monuments.

Solutions and Awareness

Air pollution can be reduced by using public transport, renewable energy and planting more trees.

Schools play an important role in spreading awareness about clean air.
